RAST SFX PACK VOL.1
Rast Creative Studio — rastcreative.com
https://rastcreative.com/en/tools/free-sound-effects-pack/

40 sound effects for short-form video editing (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, YouTube).
Format: WAV, 48 kHz, 16-bit, stereo. Leading/trailing silence trimmed, edges faded.
Levels: short sounds peak at -1.5 dBTP; long sounds (drones, long risers) about -16 LUFS.

FOLDERS (folder and file names are Turkish)
  whoosh/   short and long transition whooshes, reverse whoosh
  riser/    tension risers, endless rise (Shepard tone), short roll
  impact/   hits, punches, sub drop
  swell/    reverse cymbal, ring, logo shine
  glitch/   digital glitch, data, VHS
  ui/       click, pop, switch, keyboard
  kamera/   camera: shutter sounds (DSLR, film, real recording)
  kagit/    paper: slide, crumple, page turn, tape rip
  bildirim/ notification: ding, bell, chime
  gecis/    transition: zip, tape stop, vinyl scratch
  drone/    dark and airy underlay beds (10 s)
